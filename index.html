ABOUT ME — 01

I'm Mara — a Berlin-based graphic designer obsessed with contrast, rhythm and type. For eight years I've turned rough ideas into bold identities that make brands impossible to ignore — from startup logos to full editorial systems.

The
Approach.

Every project starts with one question: what does this brand refuse to compromise on? The answer becomes the spine of the whole visual system — the thing we protect when everything else is up for debate.

I work hands-on with founders, studios and cultural institutions. Small teams, direct lines, zero account-manager fog. Big type, sharp grids, no decoration without a reason.
